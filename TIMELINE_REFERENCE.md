## Timeline Guidelines for Remaining Book 1 Chapters

### For Chapters 39-40 (Final Resolution):
- **Next available date**: 3/6/1320
- **Recommended pattern**: 1-3 days per chapter depending on narrative intensity
- **Academy training chapters**: Often single-day intensive experiences
- **Travel/quest chapters**: May span multiple days
- **Major battle chapters**: Usually single-day but may extend to 2-3 days

### Time of Day Conventions:
- **Dawn**: Sunrise, new beginnings, preparation
- **Morning**: Active learning, meetings, planning  
- **Midday**: Peak activity, major confrontations
- **Afternoon**: Continued action, development
- **Evening**: Resolution, reflection, preparation for rest
- **Night**: Rest, dreams, secret meetings, or climactic moments
- **Sunset**: Endings, transitions, romantic moments

### Timeline Variant Guidelines:
- **Prime Timeline**: Main narrative thread
- **Divergence Point Alpha**: Alternate outcomes/possibilities  
- **Pangea Insertion**: Mythical/magical realm events
- **Timeline Fracture**: Reality breaks/paradoxes
- **Convergence Point**: Multiple timelines merging
