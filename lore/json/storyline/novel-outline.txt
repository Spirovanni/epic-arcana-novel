Opening ImageChapter 1(Intro) "Despair"- In the first chapter, we are introduced to Francisco Petrarch, a young man attending the University of Bologna in 1321. He is struggling with feelings of despair and isolation, as he feels disconnected from his peers and his own family. Francisco Petrarch attends a crowded lecture at the University of Bologna, feeling isolated and disconnected despite being surrounded by people. During the lecture, he accidentally encounters Novella d'Andrea, the daughter of Giovanni d'Andrea, who is covering her father's lecture from behind a screen. This encounter leads Francisco to question the nature of intrigue and what lies behind the screen of reality. He also introduces his Trionfi cards in a mysterious context, hinting at their unusual power and nature. He notices a feature of a card he never noticed before and it is a large arched pathway going through a city square with what appears to be two metal rails on the floor going into the tunnel. The chapter ends with the distant grumbling of a train far away.Chapter 2(Inciting Incident) " Guileless" - This chapter starts with the background of the Zanetti Train, a locomotive unbound by the conventional tracks of time, steams ahead to a reality where Petrarch's name is but a whisper lost in the winds of obscurity. This train heads through the city square square of Bologna and the mayhem begins as people question how a previously unknown transportation vehicle runs through town. Some people are surprised but others act like they have been aware of the train for years. Francisco and only Novella are aware that this train does not belong here. In the second chapter, Francisco's life is turned upside down by this train and also when he meets Dante, the famous poet and philosopher. Dante reveals to Francisco that the Divine Comedy was not just a work of fiction, but a map to different realms and dimensions in Pangaea. Dante convinces Francisco to join him on a quest to find the lost books of Cicero, aided by the Catalan Company.
